Results (Phase 2 - ROS + gazebo)

Run-1 Results:
Duration compared: 33.3s
PID publishes: 536  (~16.08 Hz)
Event-triggered publishes: 50  (~1.50 Hz)
Communication savings: 90.7%
PID steady-state mean |error|: 0.0261
Event-triggered steady-state mean |error|: 0.0295

Run-2 Results:
Duration compared: 62.0s
PID publishes: 1489  (~24.02 Hz)
Event-triggered publishes: 84  (~1.36 Hz)
Communication savings: 94.4%
PID steady-state mean |error|: 0.0177
Event-triggered steady-state mean |error|: 0.0165

Run-3 Results:
Duration compared: 101.9s
PID publishes: 2596  (~25.47 Hz)
Event-triggered publishes: 119  (~1.17 Hz)
Communication savings: 95.4%
PID steady-state mean |error|: 0.0107
Event-triggered steady-state mean |error|: 0.0103

Communication savings were consistent across three independent runs, ranging from 90.7% to 95.4%,
with tracking error (RMSE) comparable to or better than the continuous PID baseline in all cases. This
corroborates the Phase 1 Simulink result (~79.9% savings under idealized conditions) and
demonstrates that the approach holds under real message-passing overhead, physics simulation, and
sensor noise.
